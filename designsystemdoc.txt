Design System:
Primary: White (#FFFFFF) – background
Accent: Charcoal Gray (#333333) – text
Highlight/CTA: Electric Blue (#007BFF) or Turquoise (#00BFA6) – buttons, links
Secondary Accent: Light Gray (#F5F5F5) – section dividers

Poppins – Modern, clean, slightly rounded (great for tech, fashion, general retail)
Pros: Excellent readability, professional but friendly.
Example pairing:
Headings: Poppins Bold / SemiBold
Body text: Poppins Regular
